# SHARE Dashboard: Illinois Department of Public Health

A public Tableau dashboard on the Illinois Department of Public Health (IDPH) website, developed during my Graduate Public Service Internship (GPSI) as a Research Coordinator in IDPH's Division of Health Data and Policy (September 2024 to December 2025).

**Live dashboard:** [SHARE: Sharing Health Assessment Results Electronically](https://dph.illinois.gov/data-statistics/iquery/share.html)

![SHARE dashboard](SHARE_dashboard.png)

## About

SHARE (Sharing Health Assessment Results Electronically) shows the health priorities that Illinois local health departments (LHDs) identified in the community health plans they submit to IDPH through IPLAN (Illinois Project for Local Assessment of Needs).

## What the dashboard shows

* **LHD counts by health category:** how many local health departments chose each category, such as Behavioral/Mental Health, Access to Care, and Substance Abuse
* **Map of IDPH health regions and LHDs:** each department shown with its selected health categories
* **Health priorities in each category:** the specific priorities grouped under each category
* **LHDs and their health priorities:** a table of every department, its chosen priorities, and their categories

Users can filter by health category, health priority, health region, SHARE round (for example, Round 7, 2024 to 2028), and individual LHD. Clicking a bar or a department on the map filters the rest of the dashboard.
